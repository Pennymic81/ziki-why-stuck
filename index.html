Proposal · human + agent interaction

Why is this stuck?

When an issue stops moving, Ziki says why in one sentence, who it's waiting on, and offers the one action that gets it going again.

😕

The problem

Work passes between people and agents many times. When it stops, nobody can tell why or whose turn it is, and waiting agents just go quiet.

💡

The idea

Every stuck issue gets a ⏳ chip. Open it to see one plain sentence, who it's waiting on, and one button to fix it. A Stuck tab lists them all.

🤖

Agents too

Agents can ask the same question through the API, so they know who to ask instead of guessing.

Built on rules Ziki already has

No new workflow and no new permissions. The sentence comes from settings Ziki already uses, like 2 confirmations, 2 merge verdicts, 3 upvotes, 60-second offers, the 2-claim limit and approval rules.
